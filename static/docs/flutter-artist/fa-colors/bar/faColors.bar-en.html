Control & Toolbars (Bar Namespace)

The Bar Namespace focuses on directional and control components located at the edges of the application. Unlike the "containing" nature of Surfaces, Bars are "instructional" by nature.

1. When to use Bar instead of Surface?

Even though a Bar is technically a surface, it is separated to manage navigation systems such as: AppBar (top), BottomBar/Footer (bottom), or functional ToolBars.

2. Border Separation Techniques

For Bars, Borders act as essential "Technical Lines":

  • AppBar: Use faColors.border.strong at the bottom edge to separate the header from the content.
  • BottomBar: Use faColors.border.strong at the top edge to anchor the tool area.
  • Vertical Splits: Use faColors.border.subtle to divide icon groups within a toolbar.
DESIGN TIP: When placing buttons (Actions) on a Bar, consider using Ghost or Outlined buttons to keep the navigation bar clean and avoid visual conflict with the Bar's border.strong lines.
