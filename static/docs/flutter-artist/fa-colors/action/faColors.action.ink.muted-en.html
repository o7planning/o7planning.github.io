faColors.action.ink.muted

This is the text and icon color specifically designed to be displayed on faColors.action.fill.muted. This token maintains the subtlety of secondary actions, ensuring content remains legible without competing visually with more critical tasks.

SYSTEM NOTE: The pairing of faColors.action.fill.muted and faColors.action.ink.muted is ideal for "Dismiss", "Close", or reference-only status labels. Do not confuse this with faColors.ink.muted (which is intended for body text on large surfaces).

Practical Application:

  • Text inside non-essential information Chips.
  • Buttons serving "cancel" or "close" roles in complex UIs to reduce visual cognitive load.
