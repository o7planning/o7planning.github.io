Color System Overview

FlutterArtist Color System is designed to follow the natural intuition of developers. Instead of memorizing complex rules, you only need to understand the relationship between Fill (Background), Ink (Content), and Stroke (Border).

1. Standard Visual Logic (Light Theme)

In Light Theme, surfaces are generally bright, while interactive elements and text are dark.

Token Namespace Light? (Bright) Dark? (Deep)

faColors.action.ink.xxxNoYes

2. Surfaces & Content

The Surface (Large backgrounds) and Bar (Structural containers) provide the "Canvas". Because they are Light/Bright in Light Theme, they must always be paired with faColors.ink.xxx (Dark text) and faColors.stroke.xxx (Dark borders) to ensure readability.

3. Interactive Elements (Buttons, Chips, Tabs)

When creating interactive components, only use tokens from the action namespace.

  • Fill: The background color of the action (Solid/Dark).
  • Ink: The color for text or icons inside or related to that action.
  • onXxxFill: Specifically designed to be placed on top of a Fill (e.g., onPrimaryFill is a Light color for text on a Dark Primary background).

The Golden Rule of Combination:

  1. Solid Style: Use action.fill.primary (Dark) + action.ink.onPrimaryFill (Light).
  2. Ghost Style: No background. Use action.ink.primary (Dark). This works perfectly when placed on a surface or bar because those backgrounds are already Light.
Note for Dark Theme: The "Light vs Dark" logic above is simply reversed. Surfaces become Dark, and Inks become Light. The system handles this inversion automatically.
